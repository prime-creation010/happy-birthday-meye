A LETTER FROM BABA
For my dearest AnusreeWith love & blessings

My dearest daughter,

On your birthday, I wanted to leave you with something more lasting than a quick wish. So here it is: a small letter from your very own Baba.

May your days be filled with reasons to smile, people who value you, and opportunities that take you closer to everything you dream of. May you always have the courage to stand by what is right, the patience to grow at your own pace, and the confidence to recognise how much you are capable of.

When life gets a little difficult, I hope you remember that a difficult chapter does not make a difficult story. Keep learning, keep laughing, stay kind, and never be afraid to begin again.

And above all, may you always be surrounded by genuine affection, good health, peace of mind, and the sort of happiness that stays long after the candles are blown out.

With endless blessings,
Your Baba — the one with a one-year age advantage.

B
